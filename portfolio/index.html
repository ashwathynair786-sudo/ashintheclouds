About

I trained as a print designer before I ever touched a screen, and it still shows in the way I work.

Most of what I make starts on paper — a thumbnail, a colour test, a scrap of type pulled from something I found at a flea market. I like brands that admit a person built them: a slightly crooked baseline, ink that bleeds a little at the edge, a joke in the footer nobody asked for.

Lately that's meant identity systems for independent food and plant businesses, posters for a jazz night that refuses to grow up, and a running list of zines I keep promising myself I'll finish. If that sounds like your kind of project, get in touch below.
