01

Anatomy

The logo is a single lockup. The monogram's knocked-out h is also the first letter of the name, so the full lockup reads hackerearth.

  1. ContainerA 30u square with 8u continuous (smoothed) corners.
  2. The hKnocked out of the container. Its stem runs off the top edge.
  3. CursorAn underscore under the h, like a terminal prompt (h_). It's the code reference in the mark.
  4. Wordmark"ackerearth", lowercase. The monogram supplies the opening h.
  5. JointA fixed 3u gap between the mark and the wordmark. Never change it.
02

Monogram construction grid

The monogram sits on a 30 × 30 unit grid. One unit (u) = 1/30 of the mark height (X). Every value scales with it.

Mark shown as a tint so the grid reads across it. Light lines are 1u, darker lines every 5u. Orange: construction. Blue: geometric centre.

The h and cursor sit about 0.3u right of the geometric centre to balance the open right side of the bowl. Keep this offset when redrawing.

03

Lockup proportions

The wordmark's x-height band is vertically centred on the monogram, so the lowercase letters sit level with the knocked-out h.

All values in u (1u = X/30). The wordmark's x-height band (8.2–22u) centres on the mark's axis at 15u. Ascenders (h, k) reach 2.8u, below the mark's top edge.

Gap

0.1X

3u from the container edge to the bowl of the "a".

Ratio

5.33 : 1

Full lockup width : height. Master artboard is 161 × 30.

Weight

1.22×

The monogram stem (3.14u) is heavier than the wordmark stem (2.57u), so the knockout holds at small sizes.

04

Clear space

Leave at least ½X (half the monogram height) on every side. Keep text, edges and other graphics out of this zone.

The faded corner tiles are the monogram at half size. Use one to measure the clear space.

Monogram on its own (avatars, favicons, app icons): ¼X minimum on every side.

Colour

08

Misuse

Don't redraw, rearrange or decorate the logo. Always start from the master files.

✕Don't stretch or squash it
✕Don't rotate or tilt it
✕Don't recolour it or use more than one colour
✕Don't change the 0.1X gap
✕Don't rearrange the mark and wordmark
✕Don't outline it or add strokes
✕Don't add shadows, glows or effects
✕Don't fill in the h or the cursor
✕Don't place it on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
