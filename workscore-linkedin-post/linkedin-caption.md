## Caption

Your workforce is changing. Is your definition of talent changing with it?

For decades, hiring has measured two things: what people know, and how much they produce.

AI now answers almost any question in seconds. And 58% of employees already use AI regularly at work, so output volume says more about the AI than about the person.

What sets people apart today is what they can do with AI: getting from a problem statement to a solution that holds up.

That is harder than it looks:
→ 1 in 2 employees say they do not understand AI or how it is used
→ 56% have made mistakes at work from AI output they did not check
→ 40% of US desk workers received AI-generated work last month that looked polished but lacked substance

Polished is not the same as done.

Introducing HackerEarth WorkScore™, the agentic skills evaluation platform.

Candidates work with an AI agent on realistic tasks in a sandbox. We evaluate the whole session, not only the final result, across four dimensions of AI fluency:

→ Problem Analysis: did they find the real problem, or guess?
→ Agent Steering: did they steer the AI, or follow it?
→ Quality Validation: did they prove the work is right, or assume it?
→ Solution Delivery: does the result hold up under conditions they never saw?

Built for engineering and non-engineering roles alike. Every score links to the evidence behind it.
