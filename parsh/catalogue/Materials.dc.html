02 Nature as Material

Every texture has a story.

Inspired by the richness of Indian craftsmanship, we explore what natural materials can express, from the earthy character of banana bark to the delicate textures of banana paper and fabric. Beauty, for us, lives in the individuality of each material, the sensitivity of form and the interplay of light and shadow.

Banana Bark

Texture
Ridged and fibrous, with natural striations along each sheet.
Translucency
Opaque. It shapes light through silhouette, edge and shadow.
Character
Earthy browns and tans. No two sheets alike.

Banana Paper

Texture
Soft and hand-formed, with fibre strands visible in the sheet.
Translucency
Semi-translucent. It diffuses light into a warm, even glow.
Character
Ivory to honey tones, with gentle irregularity.

Banana Fabric

Texture
Woven fibre with a dry hand and a subtle sheen.
Translucency
Filters light through the weave, revealing its structure when lit.
Character
Muted natural neutrals with a quiet lustre.
