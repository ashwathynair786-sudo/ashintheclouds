03 The Art of Making

Shaped with intention.

Every PARSH piece begins in the hands of a maker.

Fibres are gathered, prepared and formed by hand, then brought together with the precision of contemporary design. The pace is unhurried, guided as much by the material as by the drawing.

What leaves the workshop carries the trace of the hands that shaped it: a living surface, a slight irregularity, a glow that could only have come from here.
