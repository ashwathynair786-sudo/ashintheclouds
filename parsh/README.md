## Logo

**Symbol: a point of light held within a leaf.** It reads three ways at once: a diya flame, a banana leaf with
its central vein, and an arched threshold. That gives you the organic and the architectural, the familiar and
the unexpected. The point of light is the only colour (Red Earth on light grounds).

**Wordmark:** PARSH in Cormorant Garamond Medium, widely spaced, with the tagline set in Jost capitals.

### pp.2–3 · 01 Philosophy: The Meaning of Light
**In India, light has always meant more than illumination.**

It marks beginnings and welcomes people home. It brings warmth to gatherings and lends a quiet beauty to the
rituals of every day.

PARSH celebrates this timeless bond between light, nature and the human spirit.

Rooted in Indian sensibilities yet shaped for contemporary spaces, our work brings together the organic and the
architectural, the familiar and the unexpected.

For us, lighting is never simply an object within a space. It is a presence, one that transforms how a space is felt.

*A celebration of nature, craft and the poetry of light.*

### pp.4–5 · 02 Nature as Material: Every texture has a story.
Inspired by the richness of Indian craftsmanship, we explore what natural materials can express, from the earthy
character of banana bark to the delicate textures of banana paper and fabric. Beauty, for us, lives in the
individuality of each material, the sensitivity of form and the interplay of light and shadow.

| | Texture | Translucency | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Banana Bark** | Ridged and fibrous, with natural striations along each sheet. | Opaque. It shapes light through silhouette, edge and shadow. | Earthy browns and tans. No two sheets alike. |
| **Banana Paper** | Soft and hand-formed, with fibre strands visible in the sheet. | Semi-translucent. It diffuses light into a warm, even glow. | Ivory to honey tones, with gentle irregularity. |
| **Banana Fabric** | Woven fibre with a dry hand and a subtle sheen. | Filters light through the weave, revealing its structure when lit. | Muted natural neutrals with a quiet lustre. |

### pp.6–7 · 03 The Art of Making: Shaped with intention.
Every PARSH piece begins in the hands of a maker.

Fibres are gathered, prepared and formed by hand, then brought together with the precision of contemporary
design. The pace is unhurried, guided as much by the material as by the drawing.

What leaves the workshop carries the trace of the hands that shaped it: a living surface, a slight irregularity,
a glow that could only have come from here.
